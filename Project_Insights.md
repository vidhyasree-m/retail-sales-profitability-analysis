# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis — Key Insights

## 1. Sales Growth vs Profit Growth

Sales increased by 23.49% in 2017, but profit increased by only 0.52%. This indicates that strong revenue growth was not translating proportionally into profit growth.

## 2. Category Profitability

Technology generated the highest sales ($716.1K) and profit ($131.2K), with an 18.33% profit margin. Furniture generated $614.7K in sales but only $16.5K in profit, resulting in a much lower 2.69% margin.

## 3. Loss-Making Sub-Categories

Tables were the largest loss-making sub-category, generating approximately $14.4K in losses. Bookcases and Supplies were also loss-making.

## 4. Discount & Profitability

Higher discount levels, particularly 30% and above, were associated with negative profitability. This suggests that heavily discounted transactions should be reviewed.

## 5. Regional Profitability

Central had the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 7.72%, compared with approximately 14–15% in the West and East regions.

## 6. Central Furniture Performance

Furniture generated a loss of approximately $2.3K in the Central region, while Furniture remained profitable in the other regions. This indicates a need to investigate the specific factors affecting Central Furniture performance.

## 7. Customer Segment Performance

Consumer generated the highest sales and total profit, while Home Office had the highest profit margin at approximately 14.53%.

# Business Recommendations

- Review transactions with discounts of 30% or higher, particularly those generating losses.
- Investigate pricing, discounting, and product-level factors behind Tables' losses.
- Investigate why Furniture is loss-making in the Central region.
- Protect the performance of high-margin categories such as Technology.
- Evaluate business performance using profitability as well as sales revenue.
